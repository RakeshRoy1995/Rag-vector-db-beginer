MICROSOFT COMPANY INFORMATION

Company Name: Microsoft Corporation
Founded: April 4, 1975
Founders: Bill Gates and Paul Allen
Headquarters: Redmond, Washington, United States
CEO: Satya Nadella
Ticker Symbol: MSFT (NASDAQ)

Overview:
Microsoft is an American multinational technology company known for software, cloud computing, artificial intelligence, gaming, and productivity solutions. The company developed Windows, one of the world's most widely used operating systems, and Microsoft 365 productivity applications.

Major Products and Services:
- Windows
- Microsoft 365
- Azure
- Microsoft Teams
- OneDrive
- Outlook
- Xbox
- LinkedIn
- GitHub
- Bing
- Surface devices

Business Segments:
1. Productivity and Business Processes
2. Intelligent Cloud
3. More Personal Computing

Mission:
To empower every person and every organization on the planet to achieve more.
